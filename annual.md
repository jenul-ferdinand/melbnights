# Annual

Big recurring festivals and events, by month. Dates shift a little each year, so fetch the url before quoting one. Checked 2026-09-22.

- Jan-Feb | Midsumma | queer arts and Pride March, St Kilda | https://www.midsumma.org.au
- Feb | Laneway | indie, hip hop, one day, Flemington | https://www.lanewayfestival.com
- Mar | Moomba | free, city, rides, Birdman Rally | https://www.melbourne.vic.gov.au/moomba
- Mar | Golden Plains | Meredith's autumn sibling, camping, one ticket for everything | https://goldenplains.com.au
- Mar | Pitch Music & Arts | techno and house, 4 days camping, Grampians, 18+ | https://ra.co/promoters/64907
- Mar | Australian Grand Prix | F1, Albert Park, a week of parties around it | https://www.grandprix.com.au
- Mar-Apr | Melbourne International Comedy Festival | 3 weeks, cheap early shows, city | https://www.comedyfestival.com.au
- Jun | Rising | arts and music, city, lots of late night stuff | https://rising.melbourne
- Oct | Melbourne Fringe | 3 weeks, cheap and weird, all over town | https://www.melbournefringe.com.au
- Dec | Meredith | camping, no lineup clashes, the Gift ballot | https://mmf.com.au
- Dec-Jan | Beyond the Valley | NYE camping festival, Barunah Plains | https://beyondthevalley.com.au
